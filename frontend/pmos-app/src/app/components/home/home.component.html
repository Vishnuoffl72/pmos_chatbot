What is PMOS?

Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS) involves hormonal imbalances affecting metabolic and reproductive health. It is characterized by a combination of endocrine system dysfunctions, leading to irregular cycles, metabolic challenges, and various physical symptoms. Early detection and lifestyle interventions are crucial.
